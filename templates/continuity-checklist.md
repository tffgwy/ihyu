# Continuity checklist

- [ ] The chapter starts from the accepted previous state.
- [ ] Every named character knows only what they could know.
- [ ] Location, time, injuries, resources, and objects are consistent.
- [ ] Power or skill use follows an established rule and pays a cost.
- [ ] The protagonist's decision follows a visible desire or pressure.
- [ ] The antagonist or opposing force makes an active move.
- [ ] Hook ledger entries are updated with evidence.
- [ ] Payoff ledger entries are updated with what was actually delivered.
- [ ] The ending changes the next chapter's available choices.
- [ ] Rejected candidate material has not slipped into canon.
