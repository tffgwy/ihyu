# Revision queue

Use this queue for accepted review findings. Keep candidate ideas separate
until a project owner accepts them.

| ID | Severity | Scope | Finding | Evidence | Required action | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REV-0001 | P1 | starter | Fill the real reader promise before drafting a book-specific chapter. | `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` placeholders | Complete the project context and promise ledger. | open |

## Severity rules

- `P0`: canon, continuity, safety, copyright, or impossible-state blocker.
- `P1`: reader promise, agency, payoff, pacing, hook, or major voice problem.
- `P2`: local prose, rhythm, repetition, and polish.
