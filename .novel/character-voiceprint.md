## Voice audit

- Do not give every character the same polished vocabulary.
- Do not make children, elders, officials, doctors, and villains speak with the
  same professional narrator voice.
- Let pressure change behavior before it changes exposition.
- A voiceprint is accepted only after it is visible in approved scenes.
